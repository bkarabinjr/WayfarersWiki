## Weapon groups

- Simple weapons
- Martial weapons
- Bespoke weapons

## Proficiency levels

- **Proficiency:** a bonus
- **Expertise:** an active technique
- **Mastery:** a passive benefit

The [[Berserker]] and [[Guardian]]'s Weapon Expertise lets them use the techniques of certain weapons.
