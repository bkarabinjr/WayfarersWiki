Potions and elixirs are consumable [[Equipment|items]].

## Drinking a potion

- **Swig ([1AP]):** roll the potion's effect.
- **Full drink ([2AP]):** the potion gives its maximum roll.

*(From the appendix of the Master Document V01.)*

## Healing potions

| Potion | Healing | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Potion of Minor Healing | 1d4 + 1 / AP | 50 gp |
| Potion of Moderate Healing | 2d4 + 2 / AP | 250 gp |
| Potion of Greater Healing | 3d4 + 5 / AP | 750 gp |
| Potion of Superior Healing | 5d4 + 10 / AP | 2,000 gp |
| Potion of Supreme Healing | 8d4 + 15 / AP | 25,000 gp |

> [!note] Check the numbers
> - "/ AP" is written after every healing amount. It probably means "per Action Point spent drinking", which would combine with the swig rule above. Confirm.
> - The price jumps from 2,000 gp to 25,000 gp for the last step, while earlier steps roughly triple. 25,000 may be a typo for 2,500 or 5,000.
> - Money and item prices depend on the wealth system, which isn't written yet (see [[Equipment]]).
