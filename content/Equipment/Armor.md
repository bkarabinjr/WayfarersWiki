Armor categories:

- Light armor
- Heavy armor
- Bespoke armor

Medium armor is being removed (see [[Defense]]).

## Armor notes

- **Heavy armor** should have some kind of drawback.
- **Super heavy armor:** −5 speed.

> [!question] Is "super heavy" a fourth armor category?
> The Additional Info notes mention super heavy armor (−5 speed). The Master Document V01 lists only light, heavy and bespoke armor.

## Shields

> [!question] How do shields work?
> - **Master Document V01** ([[Weapons and Techniques]]): +1 DV while using a shield. With the shield technique, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on an attack against someone else.
> - **Additional Info notes:** you have to spend an AP to use the shield and get its bonus to AC (DV). You can use a reaction to shield block.
>
> The [[Guardian]]'s Shield Block also uses a reaction.
