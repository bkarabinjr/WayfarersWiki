A record of design decisions, newest first. When you settle a design question, add a row here and update the pages it affects, so the question doesn't get reopened later.

## How to log a decision

1. Answer the question on its page by replacing the *Design question* box with the final rule.
2. Add a row to the table below.
3. Update any other pages that used the old rule (use search to find them).
