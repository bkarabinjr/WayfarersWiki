Before Wayfarers is shared publicly or sold, some names and text borrowed from other games should be replaced. This list covers what turned up while building the wiki. It isn't legal advice. If the game will be sold, it's worth checking with someone who knows tabletop publishing.

> [!question] Is Wayfarers for a home table or for publishing?
> For private play, borrowed names don't matter. For a public or paid release, everything below should be replaced or checked.

## Text that closely follows 5e wording

These were likely adapted from 5e and read close to the original. Rewrite them in Wayfarers' own words, which is also a chance to fit them to Wayfarers terms (DV, Awareness, AP):

- [ ] Cover and obscurement → [[Cover and Obscurement]]
- [ ] Alert, Actor, Mobile, Sharpshooter, Great Cleave → [[General Feats]], [[Skill Feats]]
- [ ] Rage, Reckless Attack, Unarmored Defense → [[Berserker]]
- [ ] Natural Explorer, Favored Enemy → [[Ranger]]
- [ ] Bardic Inspiration → [[Bard]]
- [ ] Trance, Fey Ancestry, Mask of the Wild → [[Sylvar]]

## Generic names that are fine

Folklore and mythology names like Kobold, Changeling, Dryad, Satyr, Minotaur, Centaur and Sprite aren't owned by anyone.
