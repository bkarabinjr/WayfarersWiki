**Role:** [[Class Roles|Vanguard]] · **Resource:** Harness Divinity (smites)

The Paladin analog, with more focus on defense. Defender of the weak, avenger of the fallen. A versatile armored warrior who can support the party as well as smite enemies, a hybrid of melee, healing and support magic.

With a limited number of spells, their ranged options are limited, but they excel offensively and especially defensively. Like the Paladin, they shouldn't be much of a threat at a distance: much of their gameplay is getting allies and enemies into their **aura** radius.

Primary abilities, class dice and proficiencies are still blank.

## Class features

- **Harness Divinity.** Charges that fuel smites, healing and *[a defense option]*. 4 per tier level.
- New Divine Sense
- Spellcasting
- Weapon Expertise

## Tier 1

- **Intercept Strike.** *(Trigger: an adjacent ally is attacked.)* You put your body in the way of oncoming harm to protect an ally.
- **Shield Block / Shield Bearer.** *(Trigger: your shield is raised and you would take damage from a physical attack.)* Your shield mitigates additional damage.
- **Weapon Expertise**
- **Divine Health**
- **Armor Expertise**
- **Challenge.** Provokes nearby targets.
- **Shield Bash.** Deals damage and stuns targets.
- **Prominence.** Deals damage to all adjacent targets.
- **Protect.** Reduces damage.
- **Shield Bearer.** Shield bonus.
- **Status Resistance**
- **Wrathful Smite** · **Thunderous Smite** · **Branding Smite** · **Searing Smite**
- **Defender's Resolve.** Add half your Resolve score to your Defense Value when you become bloodied.
- **Shielding Taunt**

## Tier 2

- **Blinding Smite** · **Retributive Smite** · **Ranged Smite**
- **Intercept Strike** *(upgrade)*
- **Trouble-leaving aura skill**
- **Healing Pulse.** Defeating an enemy in your aura heals you and one ally in your aura.
- **Bastion**
- **Divine Ally.** Gain a steed spirit as an attack ally, defensive ally, or support mount.
- [New Social Skill] · [New Exploration Skill] · [Upgraded Exploration Skill] · [Upgraded Combat Skill] ×2
