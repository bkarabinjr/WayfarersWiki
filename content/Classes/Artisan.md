**Role:** [[Class Roles|Specialist]] · **Mechanic:** Projects

A skilled craftsperson who makes magical items with their hands. Artisans infuse magic into what they craft and into everyday objects, often as passive magic. Artisans craft **Projects**. Marked *Advanced*.
