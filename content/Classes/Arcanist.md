**Role:** [[Class Roles|Paragon]] · **Resource:** Scripts

Previously the Wizard, the Arcanist is the quintessential arcane caster. Arcanists are scribes and scholars who seek to understand the fabric and mechanics of the arcane. They have much more freedom when casting spells of their specialty, and can **store spells for later use**.

Primary abilities, class dice and proficiencies are still blank.

## Tier 1

- **Lore Mastery.** Bonus to knowledge checks, and you can use Intelligence for initiative checks.
- **Spell Secrets.** When you cast a spell that deals toxic, cold, force, electric, necrotic, radiant or sonic damage, you can swap the damage type for another from that list.
- **Alter Saving Throw**
- *Seven open slots*

## Tier 2

- **Altered Casting** *(rename; probably just metamagics)*. When you cast a spell, you can upcast it to augment the spell:
  1. **Force.** If you roll damage, increase damage against every target by 2d10 and add force.
  2. **Range.** *Not written yet.*
  3. **Potency.** Increase the spell DC by 2.
