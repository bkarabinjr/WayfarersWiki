**Role:** [[Class Roles|Specialist]] · **Primary ability:** Wisdom

A class that excels at status effects, buffing allies and debuffing enemies. Sages are similar to clerics, but don't wear heavy armor or have any inherent weapon proficiencies. They support allies with an array of abilities, effects and enhancements, and use healing and barriers to strengthen allies' defenses. Physically weak, compensated by their specialization.
