> [!question] Subclass prefixes
> Several classes use two different prefixes in different parts of the source: Arcanist (Curriculum vs Academy), Cleric (Domain vs Prayer), Druid (Circle vs Grove), Sorcerer (Heritage vs Soul). Pick one per class.

Each class page lists every subclass name from each version of the source, plus the long expansion lists.
