Where a [[Subclasses|subclass]] is a *branch* of a class, a prestige class is a **convergent** class: a specialization multiple classes can share, or a path that different classes travel toward and meet at.

Two necromancers, one from a [[Warlock]] background and one from an [[Arcanist]] background, can lead to interesting narrative moments. There could be a whole tribe of necromancers, shamans or grove keepers, all dedicated to a similar cause and study but coming from different walks of life.

Prestige classes have their own **Prestige Class Feats** (see [[Feats]]).
