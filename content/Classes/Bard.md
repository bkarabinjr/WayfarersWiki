**Role:** [[Class Roles|Gifted]] · **Resource:** Spotlight

Performers who invoke magic through song, dance and verse. A versatile Charisma caster with lots of buffs and support options, expert at inspiring allies and disheartening foes. Their buffs also debuff. Signature ideas: **Inspiration** and **Encore** (an Encore that refreshes each [[Scenes|scene]]).

- **Primary abilities:** Charisma, Dexterity
- **Class dice:** d8

## Proficiencies

- **Ability proficiency level increase (choose two):** Dexterity, Charisma, Resolve
- **Skills:** Choose any two
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, hand crossbows, shortbows, shortswords, rapiers
- **Tools:** Three musical instruments of your choice
- **Armor:** Light armor

## Class features

**Bardic Inspiration [1AP].** Choose a creature other than yourself within 60 feet who can hear you. It gains one Bardic Inspiration die, a d6. Once within the next 10 minutes, it can roll the die and add it to one roll, check or action die. Once rolled, the die is lost. A creature can have only one Bardic Inspiration die at a time.

**Spellcasting.** *Not written yet.* See [[Spellcasting]].
