**Role:** [[Class Roles|Vanguard]] · **Resource:** Valor

Soldiers are masters of weaponry who rely on tactics and maneuverability to dominate in battle. Valor fuels their unparalleled combat prowess. They are well acquainted with death, both in dealing it out and staring it defiantly in the face. Soldiers are adept with a variety of weapons, and a well-equipped soldier always has the right tool for the job. Practitioners in the art of attrition: the longer a soldier stays in the fight, the more dangerous they become.

An armored battlefield tactician, leader and weaponmaster who commands and maneuvers around the battlefield.

- **Primary abilities:** Might, variable
- **Hit points and class die:** 1d10

## Proficiencies

- **Ability proficiencies (choose two):** Might, Dexterity, Resolve
- **Skills (choose two):** Acrobatics, Animal, Athletics, Awareness, Insight, Intimidation, Survival, Knowledge: History
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, martial weapons
- **Tools:** None
- **Armor:** Light armor, heavy armor, shields

> [!question] Valor or superiority dice?
> The class description calls the resource **Valor**. A later roles list says "[superiority dice]", and the maneuver rules use superiority dice. Decide whether Valor *is* the dice or a separate pool. See [[Soldier Maneuvers]].
