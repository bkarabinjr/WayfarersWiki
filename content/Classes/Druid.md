**Role:** [[Class Roles|Paragon]] · **Signature:** Wild Shape

A versatile primal caster, one with nature. Druids affect areas with nature spells and field effects and interact with animals.

- **Primary abilities:** Wisdom, Intelligence
- **Class dice:** d8

## Proficiencies

- **Ability proficiency level increase (choose two):** Wisdom, Intelligence, Dexterity
- **Skills (choose two):** Knowledge: Nature, Knowledge: Arcana, Acrobatics, Taming, Insight, Awareness, Survival, Influence
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons
- **Tools:** Herbalism kit
- **Armor:** Light armor

## Class features

**Nature's Gift.** *Not written yet.*

**Wild Shape [1AP].** You transform into a beast form you have learned. You stay in that form for a number of hours equal to twice your tier level, or until you use Wild Shape again, become incapacitated, or die. You can leave the form early for 1 AP.

**Primal Spellcasting.** You gain access to the Primal spell list. See [[Spellcasting]].

**Voice of Nature.** You know the secret language of druid orders. You can speak to nature by spending 10 minutes communing. You can also use Influence to make an impression on animals and make very simple requests of them. In most cases, wild animals will give you time to make your case.

Tier abilities are still the blank [[Class Template]].
