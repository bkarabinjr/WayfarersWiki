**Role:** [[Class Roles|Specialist]] · **Resource:** Schemes or Panache

A skilled, agile martial class that uses stealth and trickery to overcome enemies and obstacles. An agile, cunning skirmisher with high melee damage who lands debilitating blows and leaves status effects on enemies. Rogues can use **Schemes** to create and exploit weaknesses and attack when least expected.
