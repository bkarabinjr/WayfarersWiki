General feats are available at every tier. Each can be taken more than once, getting stronger at higher tiers.

| Feat | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Health Increase** | Increase your health by your Resolve score | ×2 | ×2 | ×2 |
| **Skill Increase** | Gain skill points equal to your Intelligence score | ×2 | ×2 | ×2 |
| **Proficiency Increase** | Raise up to two skills, learnings or weapons to *Proficient* | ×2, up to *Expert* | ×2 | ×2 |
| **Attribute Increase** | +1 to one attribute | +1 to one attribute | +1 to one attribute, or +1 to any | +1 to one attribute, or +1 to any |

"×2" means the source lists that option twice at that tier.

The [[Background Identity|Prodigy]] identity adds 2 skill points each time you take Skill Increase.

## Revised structure (Additional Info notes)

The Additional Info notes restructure general feats so each pick is a separate, repeatable option with a tier requirement:

| Feat | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Health Increase** (your Resolve score) | 3 picks | 2 picks | 2 picks | 2 picks |
| **Skill Increase** (skill points = Intelligence) | 3 picks | 2 picks | 2 picks | 2 picks |
| **Proficiency Increase** (two skills, learnings or weapons) | 3 picks, up to *Proficient* | 2 picks, +1 level up to *Expert* | 2 picks, +1 level up to *Master* | 2 picks, +1 level up to *Master* |
| **Attribute Increase** | +1 to each attribute, once each | +1 to each attribute, once each | +1 to each attribute once each, plus +1 to any | +1 to each attribute once each, plus +1 to any |

From Tier 2, a Proficiency Increase can raise the same skill twice.

The same notes list **Knight of the Crown** as a Tier 2 feat.

> [!question] Which general feat structure?
> The table at the top of this page is the Master Document V01 version (1 pick at Tier 1, "×2" at higher tiers). The revised structure above has more picks at every tier. Pick one.

> [!question] Proficiency ladder
> Tier 1 raises to Proficient and Tier 2 to Expert. Tiers 3 and 4 don't name a level in the Master Document V01; the Additional Info notes use **Master** for both. See [[Weapons and Techniques]] for the proficiency / expertise / mastery split.

## Tier 1 general feats

- **Actor.**
  - Advantage on Charisma (Influence) and Charisma (Performance) checks when trying to pass yourself off as someone else.
  - You can mimic the speech of another person or the sounds of other creatures. You must have heard the person speak, or the creature make the sound, for at least 1 minute. A successful Wisdom (Insight) check contested by your Charisma (Influence) check lets a listener tell the effect is faked.
- **Alert.** See [[Skill Feats]].
