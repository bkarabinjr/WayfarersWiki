Your Origin combines your [[Lineages|Lineage]], your [[Background Identity|Background]], and your past before you became an adventurer.

## Prompts

**Lineage and cultural origin**

- Region
- City

**Personality**

- Attitude
- Dress
- Personal style

**Things that matter to you**

- A memento or item that's important to you
- Motivations and relationships
- Prized possessions and things you value

**Your past**

- Childhood environment
- Family background
- Friends
- Enemies
- Lovers
- Trauma
- Secret
- Short-term goal
- Long-term goal

Every character should have a **flaw** and a **secret** for the GM to weave into the story, checked in on at each [[Tiers|tier]]. See [[Design Pillars]].
