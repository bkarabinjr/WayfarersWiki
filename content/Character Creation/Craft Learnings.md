Craft (___) is a trained skill. Common crafts:

- Alchemy
- Calligraphy
- Cartography
- Engineering
- Gemcutting
- Leatherworking
- Masonry
- Painting
- Sculpting
- Smithing
- Weaving
- Woodworking

The [[Stoutfolk]] also list **brewing** and **cooking** as crafts, and the [[Artisan]] class builds on crafting.
