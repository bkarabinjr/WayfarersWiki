Choosing how you level up should cut down the time a player spends scanning their sheet, because they choose each ability themselves. Players can also take fewer abilities and focus on improving each one's potency.

- Players get **two feat points** every time they level (see [[Feats]]).
- [[General Feats]] cover health, skill points, proficiency and attribute increases.
- Class abilities unlock by [[Tiers|tier]] (see [[Class Template]]).
- Tiers 2, 3 and 4 each grant an extra [[Action Points|Action Point]] in the class drafts.
