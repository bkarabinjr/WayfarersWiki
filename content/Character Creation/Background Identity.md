Pick **any two languages** to be fluent in and **any two skills** to be proficient in. Then pick one of the following identities:

| Identity | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Fighter** | Bonus to melee attacks. |
| **Marksman** | Bonus to ranged attacks. |
| **Defender** | Bonus to your Defense Value (DV). |
| **Adept** | Choose 3 Universal cantrips. |
| **Explorer** | Find food and fresh water for yourself and up to 5 people. Ignore non-magical difficult terrain. |
| **Speaker** | Sociability focused. Choose 2 extra languages to be fluent in. Proficiency in all languages. Find lodging for free in towns. |
| **Acolyte** | Allies within 10 feet of you reroll 1s on non-damaging abilities. |
| **Prodigy** | Gain 2 additional skill points whenever you take the Skill Increase feat (see [[General Feats]]). |
| **Scholar** | Knows more spells and knowledge. |
| **Courtesan** | Bonus to Perform. |
| **Agent** | Bonus proficiency. |
| **Rascal** | Increased Initiative and Stealth without reducing speed. |
| **Noble** | Knows more people. |
| **Merchant** | Bonus to appraising and bartering. |
| **Pilot** | Piloting machines and navigation. |
| **Laborer** | Fortitude and extra proficiencies. |

> [!note] Rough entries
> Scholar through Laborer are one-line ideas and need exact numbers.
