Chapter 1 of the master document walks through building a character.

1. **[[Origin]]**: your lineage, background, and life before adventuring.
2. **[[Background Identity]]**: two languages, two skills, and one identity.
3. **[[Personality and Background]]**: bond, ideal and quirk.
4. **[[Lineages|Lineage]]**: your people.
5. **[[Classes|Class]]**: your playstyle and core abilities.
6. **[[Attributes]]**: choose a [[Starting Attribute Arrays|starting array]].
7. **[[Skills and Learnings]]**

## Creation modes

The source names three levels of creation:

- **Easy create**
- **Middle create**
- **Full game**

> [!idea] Traveller-style creation
> Add a creation system like *Traveller*'s that challenges your physical, mental and spiritual sides. It could give up to a +2 boost in one area and −1 in another, and award more skill points.
