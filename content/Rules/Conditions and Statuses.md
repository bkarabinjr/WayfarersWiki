Status effects are a big part of Wayfarers combat. Many [[Damage Types]] can apply a status. The source has two lists of conditions.

## Chapter 5 conditions

- Off-Guard
- Blinded
- Charmed
- Deafened
- Frightened
- Grappled / Restrained
- Incapacitated
- Invisible
- Sleep
- Petrified
- Poisoned
- Prone
- **Slowed.** −1 Action Point and speed reduced.
- Unconscious

## Definitions from the Additional Info notes

| Condition | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Blinded** | — |
| **Deafened** | — |
| **Dazzled** | — |
| **Flat-footed** | — |
| **Death's Door / Doomed** | *New. Not written yet.* |
| **Weakened** | Decreases physical checks and saves |
| **Stupefied** | Decreases mental checks and saves |
| **Overwhelmed / Frazzled** | −1 or −2 to all saves, or disadvantage on attacks against the creature that frazzled you |
| **Dazed** | Disadvantage on perception |
| **Helpless** | Attacks against you are automatic crits; you can be killed outright (coup de grace) |
| **Concealed** | Difficult to see, but you are still observed |
| **Confused** | Can't tell friend from foe: targets random creatures or hits itself, and is flat-footed |
| **Controlled** | — |
| **Unconscious** | — |
| **Sick** | — |

The same notes list three damage-over-time **effects**: Bleeding, Poisoned and Decay (see [[Damage Types]]).

## Attribute penalties

| Status | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Drained** | Penalty to physical checks; lose HP and max HP |
| **Enfeebled** | Penalty to physical checks and melee attack rolls |
| **Stupefied** | Penalty to mental checks and spell power (the Additional Info notes say mental checks and saves) |
| **Depressed** | Penalty to spiritual checks |

## Possible groupings

Senses (fascinated, dazzled) · Weakness · Ailments · Impairments (lowered abilities) · Mental

## Also used in class text

Shaken, battered, bloodied, flat-footed (probably Off-Guard), fatigued, brittle (see [[Appendix Notes]]).

**Persistent damage** is listed as a topic.
