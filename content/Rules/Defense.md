Characters have a **Defense Value (DV)**. Several abilities raise it (the Defender identity, Beasthide, the Guardian's Defender's Resolve), and the [[Berserker]]'s Unarmored Defense sets a base **Defense Score** of 10 + Dexterity and Resolve bonuses.

## Design notes

- **Two defense systems:** one that deals with damage, one that handles status effects.
- Open questions from the notes: Being hit → Dexterity? Overcoming damage / mitigation → Might? Shrugging off effects → Resolve? Or physical, mental and shrugging off effects?
- **Removal of medium armor** (see [[Armor]]).
- **Removal of piercing damage** (see [[Damage Types]]).

> [!question] How does defense work?
> Decide on the two defense systems. "DR" (damage reduction, from the [[Khitin]]) may be the damage-side one.
