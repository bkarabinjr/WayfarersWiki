Elemental effects that can be applied to creatures.

- **Fireblight / Burned**
- **Waterblight**
- **Iceblight / Frozen**
- **Thunderblight / Paralyzed.** Fails physical saving throws, has trouble speaking, attack rolls against the creature have advantage, and *(something else)* until a certain number of Action Points are spent.
- Poisoned
- Stunned
- **Blastblight**
- **Bubbleblight**

> [!idea] Water and ice
> Water-type and ice-type abilities slow. Ice can freeze.

See [[Damage Types]] for which status each element applies.
