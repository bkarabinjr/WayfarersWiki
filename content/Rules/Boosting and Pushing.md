Attributes represent not only how strong you are, but how much energy you have in a type of ability before you exhaust yourself. You can use attribute points to **boost** rolls, which makes the same attribute number noticeably stronger in Wayfarers than in D&D.

**Pushing an attribute when you have nothing left to push gives you a level of exhaustion.** See [[Exhaustion and Wounds]].

## Why boosting?

The designer was never a fan of spammable spells like *guidance*. Boosting is a limited, attribute-based way to improve a roll.

Some abilities cost a Boost, such as [[Beastcaller]]'s Exceptional Training and Critter Dispersal.
