## The order of combat

## Combat length

| Fight | Rounds |
|---|---|
| Quick | 2–3 |
| Moderate | 4–5 |
| Boss or long fight | 6–8 |

Rounds are 15 seconds. See [[Design Philosophy]].

## Ranges

Combat doesn't need a grid. Most abilities use three ranges:

| Range | Distance |
|---|---|
| **Close** | within 30 ft |
| **Near** | 30–60 ft |
| **Far** | 60 ft+ |
