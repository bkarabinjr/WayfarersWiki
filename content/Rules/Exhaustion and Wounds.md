Exhaustion and wounds are ailments and negative effects on your character.

Exhaustion shouldn't feel like death. In an intense session, it could be common to burn out one of your attributes and gain levels of exhaustion (see [[Boosting and Pushing]]).

A more interesting health and status management system is on the [[Roadmap]].

> [!note] Related terms
> "Bloodied" (see [[Appendix Notes]]), "marking a vitality" ([[Berserker]]) and "Physical Points" ([[Soldier]]) all point toward this system but aren't defined yet.
