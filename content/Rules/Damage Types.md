Damage types are grouped into three families. Many apply a status (in brackets).

## Non-elemental

- **Sharp.** Attacks that cut, slash or pierce through objects. May cause **[Bleed]**.
- **Blunt.** Attacks that pummel, smash and deal blunt-force trauma. **[Daze]**

## Elemental

- **Fire.** May cause **[Burn]**.
- **Ice.** A chilling, frigid blast of energy. **[Freeze]**
- **Nature**
- **Water**
- **Electric.** **[Stun]**
- **Sonic**
- **Air.** Represents the constantly flowing nature of air. *(Effect unfinished: probably moves the target.)*
- **Toxic.** **[Poison]**

## Mystical

- **Force**
- **Psychic**
- **Necrotic.** **[Decay]**
- **Radiant**
- **Spirit**
- **Time.** **[Haste]** and **[Slow]**
- **Space**

> [!note] Design direction
> The notes list "Removal of piercing damage", so the old slashing/piercing/bludgeoning split becomes Sharp and Blunt. Several class drafts still use the old names and should be updated.

See [[Conditions and Statuses]] and [[Elemental Blights]].
