**[[Ranger]] subclass draft**, likely the Conclave of the Beast Tamer.

## Tier 1

- **Primal Visage.** Use an attribute from a beast in your Vessel.
- **Tamer Skill.** 2 beasts per tier level. Only one beast can be out at a time; switching beasts costs AP. Beasts have their own movement and swift action, but share AP with the ranger.
- **Blood Bond.** Beasts gain a bonus to attack creatures that attacked the ranger. Lasts one round.
- **Armor.** Beasts gain an armor bonus.
- **Gathering Critters.** *Not written yet.*
- *Three open slots*

## Tier 2

- **Exceptional Training.** More training and beast damage options: increased intelligence, a fly or hover speed for a minute, and more. Costs a Boost (see [[Boosting and Pushing]]).
- **Improved Blood Bond.** The attack bonus lasts until the creature is no longer a threat during the scene.
- **Improved Armor**
- **Beast Mastery**
- **Stampede**

## Tier 3

- Beast Share Spell upgrade
- Increased Critters ability
- Magical Beast Mastery

## Tier 4

- **Inspire Greatness.** *Not written yet.*
- **Critter Dispersal.** When you take damage, you can Boost to use a swift action: gain resistance to the damage, vanish into your critters, and reappear within 30 feet.
