The three pillars of TTRPGs (combat, exploration and social interaction) are all equally present in Wayfarers, and each can be played up or down to fit any scenario.

## Combat

See [[Combat]] and the combat sections of [[Design Philosophy]].

## Exploration and navigation

Exploration has traditionally been a dull part of the game: a series of nights taking watch shifts to see if wild creatures attack, or rolls to survive the wilderness and reach your destination. Classes like the [[Ranger]] and [[Druid]] are meant to thrive in the wilderness, so Wayfarers aims to make exploration fun and dynamic with events, [[Skill Challenges]] and more.

**Every class has at least one way to interact with the environment and explore.**

## Social interaction and roleplay

Every character should have some kind of secret or background information that they check in with **at each tier**. This gives players a reason to play through the story and to explore their character and their relationships. See [[Tiers]] and [[Origin]].
