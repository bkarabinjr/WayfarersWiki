A **scene** is a segment of gameplay: a battle, a negotiation with a noble, an exploration, an escape. Anything that can be an encounter or story beat can be a scene.

Framing play as scenes helps the GM set scenes, focus a scene on a character or event, and transition between them. It also helps track which characters are where. Some effects last for an entire scene to give it a particular feel.

Most scenes last up to about **half an hour**, but that can stretch or shrink as needed.

## Scenes as a refresh timer

Besides short and long [[Resting|rests]], scenes can refresh or activate character abilities, or allow **once-per-scene** abilities. A [[Berserker]]'s rage or a [[Bard]]'s Encore refreshing each scene fits the theme.

## Types of scene

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Conflict** | A battle, a chase, or a tense interaction between characters. |
| **Interlude** | Slower moments, like exploration or time in town, where the GM can describe time passing. |
| **GM** | No player characters present; players are spectators. Used to set the stage, transition between scenes, foreshadow events, show the villain's actions, or introduce a future threat. |

The **Leave Scene** combat action lets a character exit a conflict scene. See [[Combat Actions]].
