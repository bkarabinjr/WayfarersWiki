Part 3 of the master document.

Much of Wayfarers is designed to give the GM an easier, more streamlined experience:

- Fewer obstacles when running encounters, navigating the game, and focusing on the story.
- **Less time rolling dice** during the game.
- Multiple ways to keep track of players, actions and decisions.
- Systems to incorporate character backstories and moments into **every tier** of play (see [[Tiers]]).

The GM uses [[Scenes]] to frame the story, including GM scenes where no player characters are present.
