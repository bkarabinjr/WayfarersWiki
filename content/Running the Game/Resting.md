## Short rest

Characters can short rest **twice per day**. A short rest takes **one hour**. You can spend any Hit Die to replenish *(unfinished)*.

## Long rest

To take a long rest, a character must **consume a ration and sleep for 8 hours**. Sleep can be broken up for patrolling, taking turns on watch, and other light, routine tasks.

If a long rest is interrupted by combat, an hour of walking, casting spells, or other strenuous activity, the character must make a DC *[?]* Constitution check. On a failure, the character still consumes the ration but must restart the long rest to gain its benefits. If they rested for at least an hour, they can take the benefits of a short rest.

If the characters are in an inn or another safe place designated by the GM, they become **Well Rested**.

## Scenes

Some abilities refresh per [[Scenes|scene]] instead of per rest.
