**Wayfarers** is a setting-agnostic tabletop RPG. It aims to give players wide expression and customization through simple options, with about the same ease of play as D&D.

> Wayfarers puts characters through physical, mental and moral trials. The goal is for them to grow in meaningful ways, not to break them.

This wiki holds everything from the *Wayfarers Master Document V01* and later design notes, broken into linked pages. It lives in a Git repository, so anyone with access can edit pages directly or ask Claude to do it. See [[How to Contribute]].

## Where to start

- **New to the game?** Read the [[Mission Statement]] and [[Design Philosophy]], then [[Tiers]] and [[Scenes]].
- **Making a character?** Follow [[Character Creation Steps]], then choose a lineage from [[Lineages]] and a class from [[Classes]].
- **Running a game?** See [[Running the Game]] and [[Combat]].
- **Working on the design?** The [[Roadmap]] lays out eight phases to a finished game, with checklists; [[Roadmap Progress]] shows how far each phase has come. [[Open Questions]] collects every unresolved design decision from across the wiki.

## How pages are marked

Every page has a status, shown as a `status/...` tag at the top of the page. Click a status tag to list every page with that status.

- **Stub**: a heading or a list of names, with no rules written yet.
- **Draft**: has real content but is still being worked on.
- **Needs decision**: the source had conflicting versions. Look for the *Design question* boxes.
- **Solid**: ready to playtest.

See [[How to Contribute]] for editing tips.
