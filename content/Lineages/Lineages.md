Chapter 3. Your lineage is your people: size, speed, a choice of attribute bonus, innate traits, and usually one **bonus lineage ability** chosen from a list of sub-lineages.

## Lineage page template

Each lineage should eventually cover:

- **Visual characteristics**: hair, skin, eyes, distinctions
- **Physical characteristics**: height, weight
- **Sociocultural characteristics**: native language
- **Biology and appearance** flavor text
- **Society and culture** flavor text
- **Traits**: attribute bonus, size, speed, age, etc.
