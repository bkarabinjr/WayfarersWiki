Insectoid lineage.

- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** *not listed*
- **Attribute bonus:** Intelligence, Dexterity or Might

## Choose one

- **Strong Legged.** When you jump, you can cover a number of feet up to twice your strength score.
- **Tympanic Antennae.** You have tremorsense out to 30 feet. You can detect and pinpoint the origin of vibrations within that radius, as long as they're in contact with the same ground.
- **Wings.** You can fly as long as you are not bloodied.
- **Multilimbed.** You have four arms that you can use independently. Your smaller arms can only hold light weapons and items.
- **Exoskeleton.** Bonus to DR.
- **Stinger.** A stinger that deals piercing damage.

## Types

- Beetle
- Mantis
- Wasp
- Spider
