- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 20 feet
- **Attribute bonus:** Might, Wisdom or Resolve

**Low-Light Vision.**

**Powerful Build.** You count as one size larger when determining weight and movement.

**Savage Attacks.** When you score a critical hit with a melee attack, you can roll one of the weapon's dice one additional time and add it to the bonus damage of the critical hit.

**Relentless Endurance.** When you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can roll one of your Class Dice and regain that many hit points.

## Bonus lineage ability (choose one)

- **U'ruk: Aggressive.** For an Action Point, you can move up to your speed toward an enemy you can see or hear. You must end this move closer to the enemy than you started.
- **H'rut: Primal Intuition.** You gain a level of proficiency in two of the following skills of your choice: Taming, Insight, Intimidation, Survival, Knowledge: Nature.
