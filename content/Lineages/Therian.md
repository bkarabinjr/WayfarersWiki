- **Size:** Small or Medium
- **Speed:** 30 feet
- **Attribute bonus:** Might, Dexterity or Wisdom

**Bestial Instincts.** You gain proficiency in one of the following of your choice: Acrobatics, Athletics, Intimidation, Survival.

## Lineage ability (choose one)

- **Climber.** Gain a climb speed.
- **Glider.** You can glide if falling at least 10 feet.
- **Racer.** Your speed increases per tier level.
- **Swimmer.** Gain a swim speed, resistance to cold damage, and the ability to breathe underwater for an hour.
- **Nocturnal.** Stealth and darkvision up to 60 feet.

## Shifting [1AP]

You can assume a bestial transformation that lasts for 1 minute, until you die, or until you revert to your normal appearance with an AP. When you shift, you gain temporary HP equal to **3× your tier level**.

### Shifting options

- **Beasthide.** You instead gain temporary HP equal to 5× your tier level while shifted, and you have a +1 bonus to your DV.
- **Swiftstride.** While shifted, your walking speed increases by 10 feet. You can also move up to 10 feet as a reaction when a creature ends its turn within 5 feet of you. This reaction doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity.
- **Longtooth.** When you shift, and for an AP on later turns while shifted, you can use your elongated fangs to make an unarmed attack. On a hit, you deal sharp damage equal to 1d6 + your Might.
- **Wildhunt.** While shifted, you have advantage on Wisdom checks, and no creature within 30 feet of you can make an attack roll with advantage against you unless you're incapacitated.
