Graceful, imbued with elements of magic and nature that show in their appearance.

- **Size:** Medium
- **Speed:** 20 feet
- **Attribute bonus:** Dexterity, Intelligence or Charisma

**Low-Light Vision.** You can see in dim light as though it were bright light, and ignore low-light concealment.

**Fey Ancestry.** Magic can't put you to sleep, and you have advantage on checks to avoid or end charms and illusions.

**Trance.** Sylvar don't need to sleep. Instead, they meditate in a semi-conscious trance for 4 hours a day.

**Sylvan Proficiency.** You gain proficiency in Awareness and one Knowledge skill of your choice. You're also proficient with the shortsword, shortbow, longsword and longbow.

## Bonus lineage ability (choose one)

- **Sun: Higher Learning.** You learn an additional cantrip and gain a level of Knowledge proficiency of your choice.
- **Moon: Shadowmeld.** You have darkvision out to 60 feet and can step from one shadow to another. When you are in low light or darkness, for an Action Point, you can teleport to an unoccupied space you can see that is also in low light or darkness. You can do this a number of times equal to your tier level, and regain expended uses after a long rest.
- **Forest: Mask of the Wild.** You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by low light, foliage, mist, heavy rain, and other natural phenomena.
- **High.** *Not written yet.*
- **Eladrin.** *Not written yet.*
