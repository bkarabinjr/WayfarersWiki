- **Size:** Small
- **Speed:** 20 feet
- **Attribute bonus:** Might, Resolve or Wisdom

**Darkvision (60 ft).** You can see in darkness as if it were low light, and in low light as if it were bright light.

**Resilient.** You have advantage on checks against being frightened and poisoned.

**Nimble.** You can move through the space of any creature larger than you.

**Stout Proficiency.** You gain proficiency in a craft of your choice: smithing, brewing, masonry or cooking. Whenever you make an Intelligence (History) check related to this craft, you count as proficient in History and have advantage on the check.

## Bonus lineage ability (choose one)

- **Hill.** Gain one extra hit point each time you level up.
- **Mountain.** You have proficiency with Light and Heavy armor.
- **Tundra.** *Not written yet.*
- **Meadow.** You can attempt to hide even when you are only lightly obscured by a creature larger than you.
